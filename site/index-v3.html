PRINCIPLE 1 · IN PRACTICE

Your phone rings.
You’re busy.

In a meeting, on a scooter, with a patient. Today that call goes to voicemail, and nobody leaves voicemail.

ANSWER

Rethink answers.

It picks up on the second ring, says it’s your assistant, and asks how it can help. A natural voice, not a menu.

CONVERSE

It listens.
It thinks. It talks.

Replies land in about a second. It can look things up, take a message, or book a slot, and it never claims to have done something it hasn’t.

BRIEF

Then it tells you.

It hangs up politely and leaves one clear note: who called, what they wanted, and what to do next.

    Engineering

    The hard part was
    one audio route.

    Android 13 added system APIs that let a privileged app hear the caller and speak into the call. On paper.

    Hearing works. The caller’s voice comes off the modem as clean 16 kHz audio and streams to the voice model.

    But the official injection track routes to voice_tx, which on this Qualcomm chip never reaches the modem. The caller hears silence.

    The fix: a media track pinned to the telephony device. Android’s audio policy then picks the in‑call music path, which the modem mixes into the call. The caller hears Rethink.

    // CallBridge.java · the line that let the caller hear us AudioTrack t = new AudioTrack.Builder() .setAudioAttributes(attrs(USAGE_MEDIA, CONTENT_TYPE_SPEECH)) .setAudioFormat(pcm16(16_000)).build(); t.setPreferredDevice(telephonyTx); // policy now selects AUDIO_OUTPUT_FLAG_INCALL_MUSIC

    Promises

    Your calls.
    Your rules.

    It says it’s an assistant.

    Every call opens by telling the caller they’re talking to an assistant. Rethink never pretends to be you, and never clones your voice.

    It only claims what it did.

    Messages, bookings and hang‑ups are real actions with real confirmations. If something fails, the caller is told.

    It lives on your phone.

    Transcripts, notes and extensions are stored on the device. Only live audio goes to the voice model, for the length of the call.
